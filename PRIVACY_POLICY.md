# Privacy Policy for AI Pilot

**Effective Date:** September 10, 2026

AI Pilot ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our data handling practices for the AI Pilot Google Chrome Extension.

## 1. Single Purpose & Data Minimization
AI Pilot serves a single purpose: acting as an autonomous AI browser assistant that helps users analyze page content, execute user-directed web actions, and run scheduled automation tasks within their browser.

## 2. Information We Handle
- **User Activity:** AI Pilot inspects interactive page elements and executes click or type actions on the active tab strictly when requested by the user.
- **Website Content:** AI Pilot reads visible text and DOM labels from the current active tab exclusively to process user-initiated page summarization, Q&A, and task automation.
- **Credentials & Settings:** All custom AI provider credentials, API keys, chat history, and scheduled tasks are stored locally on your device via Chrome's `chrome.storage.local` sandbox.

## 3. Data Transfers and Third Parties
- **No Selling of Data:** We do **not** sell, rent, trade, or transfer your data to third parties.
- **Direct API Communication:** When executing an AI prompt, the necessary prompt and page context are sent directly to the AI provider endpoint chosen and configured by the user (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Groq, or local gateway).
- **No Tracking:** We do not track users across websites or collect personally identifiable information, health data, financial data, or location data.

## 4. Security
All local configurations are protected by standard browser extension isolation mechanisms.
